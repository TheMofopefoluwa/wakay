Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Wakay helps you find jobs and prepare applications. This page explains what information we keep, why, and who can see it.

What we store about you

  • Your account: your email address, and your password if you sign up with email. Passwords are handled by our login provider and are stored in scrambled form, so we cannot read them. If you use Google or GitHub, we receive your email address from them.
  • Your profile: the roles, level and locations you chose, and whether you want email alerts.
  • Your CV area, if you use it: your name, phone number, address, optional LinkedIn link, your CV text and the writing samples you paste in.
  • Your applications: the jobs you saved, their status and your notes.
  • Usage counts: the times you used the application writer, so a daily limit can be applied.

The job listings themselves are public postings collected from company career pages and job boards. They are not personal information.

How your CV is used by the AI writer

When you press the button that tailors your CV, Wakay first removes your name, email, phone number, address and LinkedIn link from your CV and writing samples. It then sends the remaining text, together with the job description, to Google's Gemini service, which writes the tailored CV and cover letter. Your real details are put back in your browser afterwards.

We use Google's free Gemini service. Google may use the text it receives to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it. You must tick a consent box before this feature works. The documents are made in your browser and are not stored by us.

Who can see your information

  • You can see it by logging in. Other Wakay users cannot see it.
  • The person who runs Wakay can technically view what is stored in the database.
  • We use these services to run the site: Supabase (database and login), GitHub Pages (the website), Google (sign-in and the AI writer) and Gmail (to send emails).

We do not sell your information and we do not show adverts.

Emails we send

We send account emails (confirming your address, resetting your password) and, if you leave the alert box ticked in Profile, emails about new jobs that match you. Untick the box in Profile to stop the job alerts.

What is kept in your browser

Your browser keeps your login session and a few choices, such as your document font and the last time you visited. Nothing is used for advertising.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account exists. To have your account and everything attached to it deleted, email us at the address below and we will do it promptly.

Contact
